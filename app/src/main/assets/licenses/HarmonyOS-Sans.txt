HarmonyOS Sans SC
=================

Files in app/src/main/res/font/ (harmonyos_sans_sc_regular.ttf,
harmonyos_sans_sc_medium.ttf, harmonyos_sans_sc_bold.ttf) are modified
(subsetted) copies of "HarmonyOS Sans SC" by Huawei Device Co., Ltd.

Modification applied for this project: the character set was reduced with
fontTools (pyftsubset) to GB2312 (6763 Hanzi) plus the characters actually
used by the app and common punctuation; no glyph outlines were altered.
Characters outside this set are rendered by the system font fallback.

Why not San Francisco (SF Pro) or PingFang SC: both are Apple-proprietary
fonts and may not be redistributed. HarmonyOS Sans SC is the closest
freely-licensable match for the iOS look (similar proportions, modern heiti),
and it is not the default UI font of any non-Huawei ROM, so the app's
appearance is consistent across devices.
